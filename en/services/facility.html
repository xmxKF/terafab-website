Facility Systems Integration

Dozens of subsystems, hundreds of thousands of monitoring points — FMCS, energy management and the digital twin bring the whole fab onto a single screen.

SCOPE · Scope of services

What we take on

S-01

FMCS facility monitoring

Fab-wide monitoring architecture, point integration, and unified communication protocols and interfaces across every subsystem.

S-02

SCADA／PLC architecture

Control network topology, server and PLC redundancy design, and security zoning for the OT network.

S-03

EMS energy management

Metering architecture for power, water and gas, consumption baselines, and energy-saving improvement analysis.

S-04

GDS gas detection

Toxic gas detector layout, evacuation broadcast zoning, and gas shut-off interlock logic design.

S-05

Life safety integration

Cross-system interlocking of fire alarm panels, emergency response procedures and pressurized refuge areas.

S-06

Digital twin

BIM×IoT data integration, building a 3D visualized facility model mapped to live equipment status.

S-07

Predictive maintenance

Vibration and current signatures capture early fault indications, establishing degradation-trend warnings.

S-08

Facility information platform

Report automation, alarm priority management, and cross-system data exchange between FMCS, MES and others.

VISIBILITY · Visualization

You can only control what you can see.

Point lists, naming conventions and data architecture left until operations begin buy you a decade of dirty data no one can analyze. TeraFab defines the fab-wide point naming convention, alarm priorities and data architecture during construction, so every subsystem lands on the same standard at handover — the operations team gets clean, usable data from day one.

METHOD · How we work

How a turnkey consultancy delivers it

01

Architecture definition

Inventory every fab subsystem and point count, then set the FMCS architecture, network zoning and data standards.

02

Point lists and specifications

Unify naming conventions, alarm priorities and protocols; issue the point list and interface specification for every system.

03

Procurement and integration management

Vendor selection, interface coordination and integrated construction management for the FMCS, EMS and GDS packages.

04

Testing, acceptance and handover

Point-by-point testing, interlock verification and platform handover, including operator training and as-built documentation.
