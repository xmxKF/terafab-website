A · Who we are

Turnkey consultancy,
one contact for the whole fab.

An advanced wafer fab is dozens of tightly coupled systems: structural micro-vibration affects the scanners, airflow design drives yield, and the OHT rail network sets the production beat. TeraFab works as a turnkey consultant, integrating every discipline and interface for the owner, so a complex fab build has just one point of contact.

P-01

Single-point turnkey integration

From requirements definition to handover, TeraFab coordinates every system consultant, designer and contractor, so the owner deals with one accountable party.

P-02

Cross-system interface management

The interfaces between civil works, cleanroom, MEP and transport systems carry the highest build risk — and are exactly where we are strongest.

P-03

Full lifecycle service

Feasibility, master planning, design integration, procurement, construction management and commissioning, extending into facility support during operation.
